# Introduction

As humans we are, programming might sound to something we cannot touch, hear or sense in any material form. Although that's true in its core concept, I find it incredible that with just code we could actually click a button and start listening to music, use a finger to unlock our smartphone, or even start a video session to speak with your friends who are living just in the other face of the planet.

Programming is that, is connecting us humans to a new whole abstraction of possibilities. We are now able to do things we didn't think we could ever do, and we do it everyday without even thinking it was not possible just a few years ago.

# Collaboration

Where's that exponential evolution coming from? That is possible thanks to multiple disciplines working together. You must understand there's no master-of-computers. Someone who's working fulltime as a programmer might not know how to create a website, and someone who develops websites may not know how to create an iPhone app. And that's ok! As I said, things are created thanks to teams working together. Without collaboration, tech world would not actually exist.

# Abstraction

Collaboration is one of the two key concepts that would sum up programming, but there's a hidden word in previous paragraph that might be the second key concept. And that word is abstraction. What do I mean by that? Easy.

Do you know how to use the device you are using to read this? I mean, you would actually have been able to browse through your device to start reading this text.

Another example: The sunset is coming close and light is starting to miss, do you know how to turn on your room lights? Maybe pressing a button? It was easy, just by pressing a button the lights turned on, don't you think?

You know how to USE the devices you have, but think about this, do you know how they actually WORK in the inside? If you don't know it's ok, that's abstraction.

By being able to forget about the details of how things are made or work, we are able to day a day easly use things it would be impossible otherwise.

Driving a car is possible thanks to abstraction, by using the public interface you are given (steering wheel, gear, pedal and brakes) you are instructing a set of chemical and mechanical procedures that generates the car movement. You don't know what is happening inside your car, you only have to care about the interface details and trust the abstraction will work fine.

But what happens when it doesn't? What happens when your car starts blinking a yellow light. Maybe if you have read the manual, you might guess that light means the driving assistance is not working properly. What to do next? Are you going to study mechanics to solve the issue? Maybe you are a musician, and studying mechanics would be something interesting. But let's be pragmatic, it's not your area of knowledge and you need your car fixed ASAP. So you trust someone with the appropiate knowledge to fix it.

That's the magic behind abstraction, just forget about the details, and start using the tools you are provided to your own benefit.
